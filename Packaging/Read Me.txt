Textual 8

Installing
Drag Textual onto the Applications folder, then open it from there. Textual
can only update itself from Applications.

You need macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

Coming from Textual 7
The first launch offers to bring over your settings, servers, scrollback, styles
and license. Nothing in Textual 7 is moved or changed by the import.

Trying and buying
Each Mac gets one 30-day trial, with no account and no card. A license key works
on 2 Macs at a time.
